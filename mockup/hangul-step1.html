Before you start

Why Hangul is easy to learn

King Sejong created Hangul in 1443 so everyone could read. The letters are not random: their shapes show how you make each sound.

Consonants draw your mouth

  • ㄱ

    tongue back

  • ㄴ

    tongue tip

  • ㅁ

    closed lips

  • ㅅ

    teeth

  • ㅇ

    throat

Vowels come from three symbols

  • ㆍ

    sky 天

  • ㅡ

    earth 地

  • ㅣ

    person 人

Add a short stroke (the old “sky” dot) to ㅣ and you get ㅏ. Add it to ㅡ and you get ㅗ.

A vowel can't stand alone in writing. It always gets the silent consonant ㅇ in front, so ㅏ is written 아.
