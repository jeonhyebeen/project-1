Lesson 1

Words in every song 노래 단어

Tap ▶ to hear it.

Songs use casual speech (반말), because they sound like talking to someone close. That is why you won’t see many “-요” endings here.

사랑해

Casual

saranghae

I love you

보고 싶어

Casual

bogo sipeo

I miss you

Literally “I want to see you.”

너

neo

you

우리

uri

we, us, our

Koreans say 우리 where English says “my”: 우리 엄마, “our mom”.

heart, mind

Lesson 2

Fan talk 팬 용어

Tap ▶ to hear it.

Words you will see in comments, livestreams and fan cafés.

최애

choeae

Favorite member (bias)

Short for 최고로 애정하는, “loved the most”.

막내

maknae

Youngest member

Used in families too. The 막내 is often teased and protected.

컴백

keombaek

Comeback

Any new release, even if the group never went away.

오빠 · 언니

oppa · eonni

Older brother · older sister

What women call older men and women. Men say 형 (hyeong) and 누나 (nuna).

대박

daebak

Awesome! / Jackpot!

Big surprise, good or shocking. Very casual.

화이팅

hwaiting

You can do it!

From English “fighting”. Cheer for anyone before a big moment.

Lesson 3

Read a line, word by word 한 줄 읽기

Tap ▶ to hear it.

Each colored word below matches its meaning underneath. These are example lines written for this lesson, in the style of a ballad chorus.

너를 보고 싶어 오늘 밤

neoreul bogo sipeo oneul bam

“I want to see you tonight”

  • 너를you (the one I…)
  • 보고see
  • 싶어want to
  • 오늘 밤tonight

우리 꿈은 영원히 빛나

uri kkumeun yeongwonhi binna

“Our dream shines forever”

  • 우리our
  • 꿈은dream (is)
  • 영원히forever
  • 빛나shines

Grammar spotted: little tags after nouns

를 marks what you see or love, and 은 marks the topic. English uses word order for this. Korean uses these small tags, so singers can move words around to fit the melody.
